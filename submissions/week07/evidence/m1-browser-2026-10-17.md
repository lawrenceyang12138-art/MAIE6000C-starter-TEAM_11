## What this proves

The browser path

```text
choose authorized root
→ receive durable scan job
→ worker scans and parses
→ keyword search over current chunks
→ open version-bound page evidence
```

is live. The remaining limitations are explicit: the UI currently reports
keyword-only search when semantic vectors are unavailable, and the browser
screenshot must be attached to the final submission package by the team.
